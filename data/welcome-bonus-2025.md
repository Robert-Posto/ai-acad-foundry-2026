Note: this document describes the 2025 edition of the welcome bonus, which
has been replaced. See the 2026 edition for the terms currently in effect.

Under the 2025 welcome bonus, new customers who opened a Digital or Premium
account and made at least one card payment within 30 days of activation
received a one-time bonus of 50 lei, credited to the account within 10
business days of meeting the condition.

The 2025 bonus was open to Romanian residents only, and each person could
claim it once, tied to their national identity number. It was not available
to Student or Basic account holders, and could not be combined with any other
Libra Bank promotion active at the time.
