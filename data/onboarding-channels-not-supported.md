---
title: Onboarding channels Libra Bank does not offer
product: onboarding
audience: retail
effective: 2025-06-01
version: 1
---

Libra Bank supports exactly three channels for opening an account: the
mobile app, a branch visit, and, for co-signed applications requiring a
guardian, a video call arranged through the app.

Libra Bank does not offer account opening by phone call. Customer support
cannot open, verify, or activate a new account over the phone under any
circumstance, even with a existing customer vouching for the applicant; this
is a deliberate control to prevent identity fraud over voice channels.

Libra Bank also does not offer onboarding through third-party comparison
websites or agents — every application must originate directly in the app
or at a branch, so that identity verification is performed by Libra Bank
itself rather than an intermediary.
