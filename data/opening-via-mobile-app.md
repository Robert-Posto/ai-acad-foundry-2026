Opening a Libra Bank account through the mobile app takes about ten minutes
and follows these steps in order:

1. Download the Libra Bank app and tap "Open an account."
2. Enter your phone number. You will receive a one-time code by SMS; enter it
   to confirm the number.
3. Photograph the front and back of your ID document. The app checks that the
   document is not expired and that the photo is readable before continuing.
4. Complete a short liveness check: turn your head as shown on screen, so the
   app can confirm you are a live person and match your face to the ID photo.
5. Fill in your personal details that were not read automatically from the
   document: email address, occupation, and the purpose of the account
   (salary, savings, or other).
6. Review and electronically sign the account agreement and the terms and
   conditions. The signature step requires a second SMS code.
7. Choose your account type (see the account types comparison document) and,
   if eligible, opt in to the current welcome bonus.
8. Wait for verification, usually instant but occasionally up to one business
   day if the automated check needs manual review. You receive a push
   notification when the account is active.

Once the account is active, the next steps are activating your debit card and
setting up online banking access, covered in separate documents.
