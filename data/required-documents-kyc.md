Libra Bank must verify every new customer's identity before an account can be
used, in line with anti-money-laundering regulation. The following documents
are accepted:

- A valid Romanian identity card (CI) or an EU/EEA national ID card.
- A valid passport, for applicants without a national ID accepted above.
- One proof of address (only required for non-resident applicants): a utility
  bill, a rental contract, or a bank statement, no older than 3 months.

Documents are submitted either as a photo through the mobile app or in person
at a branch. Photocopies without the original document present are not
accepted at a branch. Expired documents are rejected automatically by the
app's verification step, even if the expiry is only a few days past.

Self-employed applicants opening a business account must additionally provide
proof of registration (Certificat de Înregistrare) and, if applicable, proof
of the beneficial owner's identity.
