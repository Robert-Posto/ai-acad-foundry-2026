---
title: Setting up online banking access
product: onboarding
audience: retail
effective: 2025-06-01
version: 1
---

Accounts opened through the mobile app have online banking (app access)
enabled automatically from activation — no separate setup is needed.

Accounts opened at a branch require a one-time online banking setup: the
branch representative issues a temporary access code, and the applicant
downloads the app and enters this code along with their phone number to
create a permanent login PIN. This temporary code expires after 48 hours; if
unused, a new one must be requested at any branch or by calling customer
support.

Online banking access includes viewing balances and transactions, moving
money between own accounts, setting up standing orders, and managing card
settings. Larger actions, such as increasing an overdraft limit or closing
an account, require additional identity confirmation through the app even
for existing logged-in sessions.
