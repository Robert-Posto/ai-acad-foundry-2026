---
title: Fees for opening a Libra Bank account
product: onboarding
audience: retail
effective: 2026-01-01
version: 2
---

Opening a personal account at Libra Bank through the mobile app is free of
charge: the account opening fee is 0 lei.

Opening a personal account at a branch, with paper forms instead of the
tablet-based signature, carries a processing fee of 25 lei, charged once the
account is activated. This fee is waived for applicants aged 65 and over, and
for Student accounts regardless of channel.

There is a minimum initial deposit of 50 lei for Basic, Digital, and Premium
accounts, which must be transferred or deposited within 30 days of account
activation or the account is automatically downgraded to a restricted,
card-only state. Student accounts have no minimum initial deposit
requirement.

These fees are separate from the ongoing monthly account fees listed in the
account types comparison document.
