---
title: Activating your debit card
product: onboarding
audience: retail
effective: 2025-06-01
version: 1
---

Once your Libra Bank account is active, your debit card must be activated
before its first use, even if it was handed to you physically at a branch.

To activate a card issued through the mobile app: open the app, go to "My
cards," select the new card, and tap "Activate." You will be asked to set a
4-digit PIN. The card is usable for online and contactless payments
immediately, and for chip-and-PIN and ATM withdrawals as soon as the PIN is
set.

To activate a card received by mail: call the activation line printed on the
sticker attached to the card, or activate it from the app the same way as
above. Cards not activated within 90 days of being issued are automatically
cancelled and must be reordered.

Virtual cards, issued only to Digital account holders, are active for online
payments immediately upon account opening and do not require a separate
activation step.
