Self-employed individuals (PFA) and small business owners can open a Libra
Bank business account. Unlike personal accounts, business onboarding cannot
be completed entirely through the mobile app: identity verification can
start in the app, but the application must be finalized at a branch, because
a representative needs to review the registration documents in person.

Required documents: a valid personal ID, the Certificat de Înregistrare (or
equivalent registration proof), and, for businesses with more than one
associate, identification for each beneficial owner holding 25% or more of
the business.

Business accounts carry a monthly fee of 35 lei, have no free-of-charge tier,
and are not eligible for any personal-account welcome bonus. Processing
typically takes 2 to 3 business days after all documents are submitted,
longer than the personal account flow, because of the manual registration
review.
