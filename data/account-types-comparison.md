Libra Bank offers four personal account types. All figures are monthly unless
stated otherwise.

| Account      | Monthly fee | Debit card       | Free ATM withdrawals/month | Overdraft limit |
|--------------|-------------|-------------------|------------------------------|------------------|
| Basic        | 0 lei       | Standard          | 3 (Libra ATMs only)          | none             |
| Digital      | 5 lei       | Virtual + physical| 5 (any bank, in Romania)     | none             |
| Student      | 0 lei       | Standard          | Unlimited (Libra ATMs only)  | 500 lei          |
| Premium      | 25 lei      | Metal             | Unlimited (any bank, worldwide) | 5,000 lei     |

The Basic account is the default account type when no other product is
requested during onboarding. Upgrading between account types can be done at
any time from the mobile app, with no fee for the upgrade itself.
