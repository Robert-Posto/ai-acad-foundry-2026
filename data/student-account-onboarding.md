The Student account is available to applicants aged 14 to 26 who are
enrolled in a recognized secondary school, university, or equivalent
program. It has no monthly fee and no minimum initial deposit (see the
account types comparison document for the full feature set).

Onboarding follows the same mobile app or branch process as any other
account, with one addition: proof of enrollment is required, either a valid
student ID photographed during the app flow or a physical student ID shown
at a branch. Proof of enrollment must be renewed once a year; the app sends
a reminder 30 days before the annual deadline, and the account is downgraded
to a Basic account automatically if it is not renewed.

Applicants aged 14 to 17 still require a parent or legal guardian co-signature
regardless of student status, per the general eligibility criteria.

Student accounts are not eligible for the welcome bonus described in the
welcome bonus documents.
