---
title: Who can open a Libra Bank account
product: onboarding
audience: retail
effective: 2026-01-15
version: 3
---

To open a personal account at Libra Bank, an applicant must meet all of the
following:

- Be at least 14 years old (see the separate minimum age policy document for
  the history of this rule — it changed recently).
- Hold a valid government-issued photo ID: a Romanian identity card (CI), a
  Romanian passport, or, for foreign applicants, a passport or national ID
  card from an EU/EEA member state.
- Provide a valid postal address. Romanian residents may use their ID card
  address; non-resident EU/EEA applicants must additionally provide a proof
  of address dated within the last 3 months (a utility bill or bank
  statement is accepted).
- Not appear on any active sanctions or fraud-prevention list checked during
  onboarding.

Non-EU/EEA residents cannot open a personal account through standard
onboarding; they must contact a branch for a manual eligibility review.

Applicants aged 14 to 17 require a parent or legal guardian to co-sign the
application, either in person at a branch or via video call — this cannot be
completed through the mobile app alone.
