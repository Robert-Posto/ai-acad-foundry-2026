---
title: Cooling-off period and closing a newly opened account
product: onboarding
audience: retail
effective: 2025-06-01
version: 1
---

Libra Bank gives every new customer a 14-day cooling-off period after account
activation, during which the account can be closed free of charge and in
full, with no explanation required, in line with standard consumer banking
protection.

To close an account during the cooling-off period, the customer opens the
app, goes to "Account settings," and selects "Close account," or requests
this at any branch. Any remaining balance is transferred to another account
specified by the customer, or issued by bank transfer if no other account is
given. Any welcome bonus already credited during the cooling-off period is
deducted before the balance is returned.

After the 14-day period, closing an account is still possible at any time
but no longer counts as a cooling-off cancellation — ordinary account closure
terms apply instead, including a requirement that the debit card be
deactivated first.
