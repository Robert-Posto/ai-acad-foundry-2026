---
title: Opening an account at a branch
product: onboarding
audience: retail
effective: 2025-09-01
version: 1
---

Applicants who cannot or prefer not to use the mobile app can open an account
at any Libra Bank branch during business hours. No appointment is required,
but booking one through the app or website reduces waiting time.

What to bring: a valid ID document (see the required documents document), and
proof of address if you are a non-resident applicant. Applicants aged 14 to
17 must bring a parent or legal guardian, who will also need to present ID
and co-sign the application.

At the branch, a representative enters the applicant's details, scans the ID
document, and takes a photo for identity verification. The applicant signs
the account agreement on a tablet. The account is typically active within
one hour, and a physical debit card is either handed over immediately, if
stock is available at that branch, or mailed within 5 business days.

Opening a business account in person is required for beneficial-owner
structures that the mobile app onboarding flow does not support (see the
business account onboarding document).
