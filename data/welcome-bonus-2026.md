This document describes the welcome bonus currently in effect, replacing the
2025 edition.

New customers who open a Digital or Premium account and make at least one
card payment within 30 days of activation receive a one-time bonus of 75
lei, credited to the account within 5 business days of meeting the
condition.

Unlike the 2025 edition, the 2026 bonus is open to any applicant who meets
the general account eligibility criteria, including non-resident EU/EEA
applicants — not Romanian residents only. It remains unavailable to Basic and
Student account holders, and each person may still claim it once, tied to
their identity document number. It cannot be combined with any other Libra
Bank promotion active at the time of account opening.
