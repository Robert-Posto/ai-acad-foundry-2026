title: Minimum age to open an account — policy history
product: onboarding
audience: retail
effective: 2026-01-01
version: 2
---

The minimum age to open a Libra Bank personal account, with parent or legal
guardian co-signature, is currently 14 years old. This has been in effect
since January 2026.

Before that date, from March 2025 to December 2025, the minimum age was 16
years old. Applications from 14- and 15-year-olds submitted before January
2026 were rejected under the older policy.

Applicants below the current minimum age of 14, regardless of guardian
consent, cannot open any Libra Bank account. There is no exception process
for this.

This is the authoritative version of the age policy; any other document
referencing "the minimum age" should be read together with the effective
date in this one.
